Why AquaGrid

India's western coast feeds millions — yet fishers still hunt blind, and fragile ecosystems go unmonitored. AquaGrid turns raw ocean data into decisions.

Fishers need PFZ

Fuel is expensive and the sea is vast. Predicting Potential Fishing Zones saves time, fuel and lives while improving catch per trip.

Biodiversity matters

Hotspots of species richness must be tracked to protect breeding grounds and keep fisheries sustainable for the next generation.

Ocean intelligence

Currents, SST, salinity and chlorophyll define where life gathers. Fusing them into one live model is the heart of ocean intelligence.

Project Objectives

Predict & Advise

Visualise Live

Answer Questions
